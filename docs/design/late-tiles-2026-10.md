# Cinderworks and Hollowglass conversion, 2026-10-24

Baseline: `a0f50bc`, after tiled Mycelium and the lantern-cap/Cerberus-rhythm changes. This adds authored three-lane layouts and tiles to floors 5-7. It changes replay rules. No combat/AI, prices, loot amounts/chances, light-cap, potions, step costs, boss HP/strike/recovery, pool splits or payout caps changed.

## Geometry and art

- Floor 5 retains its four-room cross; floor 6 its five-room hub, with the former painted furnace obstacle replaced by open central floor. Every corridor and doorway is three cells wide. All exits have eight floor neighbours and the same existing spawn exclusion (Chebyshev >2). Props still cannot cut off any floor tile.
- Walkable area: **110 → 171** (5), **85 → 171** (6), **121 → 169** (7). Dimensions remain 27x17. Stairs stay at (22,8)/(23,8)/(13,3). The arena rift moves one cell inward, (13,14) → (13,13), to have floor below it.
- Floor 7 is a clear **13x13 arena**, x=7..19/y=2..14. Cerberus occupies (12,7)..(13,8), between the rift and stairs. A 6x6 fight area around its 2x2 body is reserved **before items spawn**; no item, prop or ordinary creature can start in the two surrounding lanes. This prevents random chests from removing the boss spawn. All eight body-edge danger cells have a safe one-step escape. HP 150, 40-light strike, one warning turn, two recovery turns, Ward and half-speed movement are unchanged.
- Both slab atlases use Catacombs' 7.5% inset/30x30 slab/1px dark joint. Rock tops crop inward by another 2.5%. Hollowglass faces retain transparent crystal silhouettes and overlay opaque tops. The bright crystal wall gate is two tiles tall. Details: `docs/art/late-tiles.md`.
- No floor loads a painting. Original PNGs and final painted WebPs are retained byte-for-byte in `art/maps/{original,painted}/`; none are in `public/` or the site build. The painting loader/caches and ART_PATCHES/ART_DOORWAYS paths were removed. The authored masks were renamed `src/game/floor-layouts.ts`, and overlays now render actual tiles.
