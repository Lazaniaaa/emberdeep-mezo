# Catacombs corridor width checkpoint, 2026-10-23

Baseline: `8249147` (tiles over the old narrow grids). This checkpoint changes gameplay geometry, not just rendering. The site and signing server must be rebuilt and released together; neither was pushed or deployed.

## Layout

All four corridor segments are exactly three floor cells across, centred on their old axes, including room doorways:

| Floor | Axis | Three-lane floor strip |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Horizontal, y=10 | x=10..21, y=9..11 |
| 2 | Horizontal, y=9 | x=10..14, y=8..10 |
| 2 | Horizontal, y=10 | x=20..24, y=9..11 |
| 2 | Vertical dead end, x=17 | x=16..18, y=12..14 |

Room links and exits retain their coordinates. The old stone stairwell outlines are now floor: stairs (26,10)/(28,9) and rifts (5,10)/(5,9) have a full 3x3 floor patch. No adjacent brick face can surround either marker. The existing renderer still puts brick faces only on walls directly above floor, rock tops elsewhere; no art or rendering rules were changed.

The spawn algorithm is untouched. Existing 200-seed tests per floor still enforce connected floor, one stairs/rift, spawn exclusion within Chebyshev distance 2, no overlaps and prop-free routes. New tests check every corridor cross-section and its straight rock borders, all three doorway lanes, a route around a single blocked centre cell, and all eight floor neighbours around exits. Floors 3-7 retain their grids, spawns and byte-identical fixed-time render snapshots.

## Measurements, before and after

Same deterministic bots and seeds, default oil multiplier 1. These are simulations, not player outcomes. Before values are from the reports committed at `8249147`; after values are regenerated by `npm run economy` and `npm run tuning`. Figures below retain the reports' rounding.

| Economy collector, 80 seeds | Gold/cent before → after | Mean deepest before → after | Deaths before → after |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Unperked, 1 flask | 4.34 → 5.18 | 1.9 → 2.2 | 1% → 0% |
| Unperked, 2 flasks | 6.04 → 5.88 | 2.8 → 2.8 | 3% → 5% |
| Unperked, 3 flasks | 6.71 → 6.67 | 3.4 → 3.5 | 1% → 3% |
| Strongest, mature locked, 3 flasks | 13.35 → 12.88 | 5.9 → 5.4 | 15% → 13% |

Two-flask unperked gold/cent fell about 2.6%; the more substantial shift is that the first flask now reaches more loot. Across the report's 80 seeds the second flask's efficiency advantage fell from about 39% to 14%.

For the chosen tuning's newcomer diver (40 seeds), 3-flask mean deepest is 5.6 → 5.6 at report precision; 5-flask mean is 6.4 → 6.2. Boss-floor arrival is 60% → 50% with 3 flasks and 68% → 65% with 5. These diver bots continue until death, so their death rate is not a player-survival estimate.

`FIELD_GOLD_PER_CENT` remains **6.4**: its mixed-crowd calibration test passed. Prices, loot, step costs, enemy stats and bot logic are unchanged at this checkpoint.
